Blocky Tales — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Blocky Tales is an internal tool used by a small studio to publish its own videos to its own TikTok account.

What we access. When the studio owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives basic account information (open ID, display name) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account.

How we use it. Only to upload and publish the studio's own videos and check their publishing status.

Storage. Access tokens are stored only on the studio owner's computer and are never shared or sold.

Other users. The app does not collect any data from other TikTok users or viewers.

Deletion. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions); tokens are then deleted.
